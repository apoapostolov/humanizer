# Narrative architecture

Load when drafting or revising fiction, stories, or long narrative prose.
Surface style cleanup does not hide plot, theme, time, or cast tells.
Fix architecture first, then discourse flow, then word choice.

This is editorial guidance adapted from StoryScope-style research. It is
not a classifier, not a fingerprint report, and not a detector target.

Aim at a human band. Inverting every tell creates a new fingerprint.
Enact 3 to 5 human-leaning moves plus exactly one rarity move. Do not
accumulate the whole list.

## Architecture

- Leave slack: ordinary sentences, an underdeveloped thought, a plain
  paragraph.
- Do not have the narrator state the lesson. Cut "it was then she learned."
  Let dialogue argue about wants, not philosophy.
- Near-total thematic unity is a tell. Let one scene exist for texture
  alone.
- Echo-test turning points: if twenty regenerations of the premise would
  produce the same turn, replace it with a turn that needs this story's
  particulars.
- Do not default to protagonist-choice plus internal-acceptance plus
  growth. Change at least one leg of that tripod.
- End one beat earlier than feels complete. Cut denouement that settles
  every account.
- Use moderate anachrony to stage information. Back-load the biggest
  disclosure. Cut front-loaded briefing.
- Do not execute show-don't-tell as dogma. Mix behavior, plain emotion
  labels, and embodied sensation. Reserve embodied for peaks. Ration
  smell. Weather can be weather. Prefer action-space setting over
  mood-sensed setting.
- Bring the protagonist on in action or dialogue, not appearance-and-backstory.
- Keep the cast graph sparse and net-neutral. Give the antagonist a
  relationship that does not involve the hero.
- Name real, correct works, places, and brands when the story needs them.
  Vague "a famous poet" is machine caution. Ask rather than invent.

## Discourse

- Run a QUD pass: list the implicit question each paragraph answers.
  Replace briefing -> justification -> consequences -> reflection with
  comparison, verification, or an earned digression.
- Outline test: if first sentences of every paragraph form a clean
  summary, the structure is machine-shaped.
- Put an unpredictable event in the middle third. Vary adjacent texture.
  Do not accelerate past the interesting part.
- Position tells survive paraphrase: ragged paragraph lengths, quotes
  not always closing a paragraph, lists not always three.

## Guard

- Invented detail is the fiction task. Still do not invent in nonfiction.
- Do not restore contractions, fillers, or hedges as a default "human
  register."
- Do not print authenticity scores.
- For a full manuscript, also load `book-writer.md`.
